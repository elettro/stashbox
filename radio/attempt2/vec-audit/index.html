What “greenlit video” means

A video counts only when its source is enabled, its asset is active, its video ID is included by the recipe or allowed by a legacy all-assets rule, and it is not explicitly excluded. Artwork-only recipes are reported separately. This audit measures configuration coverage. It does not claim that every remote file is browser-playable.
